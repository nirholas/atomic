# Code of Conduct

## TL;DR

Be useful. Be honest. Don't be cruel.

## Expected behavior

- **Engage with substance.** Critique ideas, not people.
- **Cite specifics.** "This breaks at `src/collect-jito.js:42`" is more useful than "this is broken".
- **Assume good faith.** Most surprising behavior is a misunderstanding, not malice.
- **Acknowledge mistakes.** Including your own. Fix them publicly.
- **Help newcomers.** Everyone starts somewhere. A clear answer is worth more than a clever one.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, doxxing, slurs, or threats — directed at anyone, including absent third parties.
- Sustained disruption: spamming issues, hijacking unrelated threads, refusing to drop a dead argument.
- Sharing or soliciting private keys, seed phrases, or other credentials of users.
- Coordinating fraud, rug-pulls, market manipulation, or any activity designed to defraud holders of any token, on-chain or off-chain.
- Doxxing the on-chain activity of identified individuals without their consent. Wallets on-chain are public; mapping them to a person's real name without permission is not.

## Enforcement

For violations, contact the maintainer via GitHub direct message or by opening a private security advisory (`Security` tab → `Report a vulnerability`). Reports are confidential. Consequences range from a private warning to indefinite ban from the repo, depending on severity and pattern.

The maintainer has the final call. Disputes can be raised in a follow-up issue if you think a decision was wrong, but expect the bar to be high.

## Scope

This applies to all spaces this project operates in: GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, the project's Telegram / Discord (when those exist), and any other forum where you're identifiably representing the project.
